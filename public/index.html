  • Cuy is the Spanish word for guinea pig.
  • Guinea pigs are native to the Andes in South America.
  • Cuy are often kept as pets and are also considered a delicacy in some countries.
  • They communicate using a variety of sounds, including squeaks and purrs.
  • Guinea pigs have continuously growing teeth and need to chew to keep them trimmed.
